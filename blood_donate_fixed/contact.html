Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about blood donation, eligibility, and the LifeFlow camp experience.

Absolutely. Blood donation is completely safe. We use only sterile, single-use equipment for every donor. A registered medical professional performs a health screening before donation. The human body replaces donated blood within 4–6 weeks.
The actual blood draw takes about 8–10 minutes. However, including registration, health screening, and post-donation refreshments, plan for approximately 45–60 minutes total.
Eat a healthy meal 2–3 hours before donating. Stay well-hydrated. Avoid alcohol and fatty foods for 24 hours beforehand. Get adequate sleep. Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that can be rolled up easily.
Whole blood can be donated every 3 months (90 days). Platelets can be donated every 2 weeks. Plasma can be donated every 4 weeks. Double red cell donations are allowed every 4 months.
It depends on how well the condition is controlled. Well-controlled Type 2 diabetes (without insulin injections) may be eligible. Our medical team will assess during the on-site health screening. Please disclose all conditions during registration.
All donors receive: a complimentary health screening report, post-donation refreshments and snacks, an official LifeFlow Donor Certificate, a health care kit, and priority blood access for yourself and immediate family members for 1 year.
Call our 24×7 helpline at +91 1800-200-25663 for immediate assistance. You can also email donate@lifeflow.org.in with the subject "URGENT" and provide the blood type, hospital name, patient details, and your contact number.
