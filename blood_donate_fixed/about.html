Our Story

About LifeFlow Foundation

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to bridging the gap between blood donors and those in critical need — one donation at a time.

Our Mission

Saving Lives Through Voluntary Blood Donation

Founded in 2010, LifeFlow Medical Foundation was born out of a simple yet urgent truth — thousands of patients in Tamil Nadu die every year due to unavailability of blood at the right time.

We organize regular blood donation camps across the state, partner with government hospitals, and maintain a real-time database of active donors who can be called upon in emergencies.

To date, we have facilitated over 12,500 life-saving transfusions, organized 45+ camps, and built a network of 3,200+ active donors.
