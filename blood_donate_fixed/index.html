Why It Matters

Every 2 Seconds, Someone Needs Blood

Blood donation is a simple act that can save up to three lives per donation. Your 30 minutes could mean the world to someone.

🚨

Urgent Need: O-Negative Blood

Our blood bank is critically low on O-Negative (universal donor) blood. If you are O-Negative, your donation is especially life-saving right now.

🏥

Safe & Medically Supervised

All donations are performed by certified medical professionals in a sterile environment. New needles are used for every donor — 100% safe.

⏱️

Only 30 Minutes

The actual blood donation takes just 8–10 minutes. Including registration and health screening, the full process is about 30 minutes.

💪

Good For Your Health

Regular blood donation helps reduce harmful iron stores, stimulates blood cell production, and provides a free mini health check-up.

🎁

Donor Benefits

All donors receive a complimentary health kit, refreshments, a donor certificate, and priority blood access for themselves and family members.
