Who Can Donate

Eligibility Criteria

Check if you meet the requirements before visiting a LifeFlow Blood Donation Camp. Use the eligibility checker below.

Requirements Checklist

  • ✓Age between 18 and 65 years old
  • ✓Body weight of at least 50 kg (110 lbs)
  • ✓Hemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL (women) or ≥ 13.0 g/dL (men)
  • ✓Blood pressure: 90/60 – 180/100 mmHg
  • ✓Pulse rate: 60 – 100 beats per minute
  • ✓No active infections or illnesses on donation day
  • ✓At least 3 months since last whole blood donation
  • ✓Had a good meal 2–3 hours before donating
  • ✗Pregnancy or breastfeeding (temporary deferral)
  • ✗Recent surgery or major dental work within 6 months
  • ✗History of HIV, Hepatitis B/C, or other bloodborne diseases
  • ✗Taking blood thinners or certain antibiotics
